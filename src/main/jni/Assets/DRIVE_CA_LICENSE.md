# Drive HTTPS trust bundle

`drive-ca.pem` is the unmodified Mozilla CA certificate bundle downloaded from <https://curl.se/ca/cacert.pem> on 2026-11-14. Its source snapshot is dated 2026-11-05.

SHA-256: `427c064216761a1e72a8b30f0d954d08cb6e4435a82091fb865afd20c8497285`.

Mozilla's source certificate data is available under the Mozilla Public License 2.0: <https://www.mozilla.org/MPL/2.0/>. Source and extraction details: <https://curl.se/docs/caextract.html> and the source URL retained at the beginning of the bundle.

The Android build embeds the bundle and writes it into the app-owned Drive cache. The Drive client verifies both the certificate chain and HTTPS hostname. Keep the source notices when updating the bundle, record its date/hash here, then rebuild.

The UI sound cues are original procedural PCM generated by `System/Audio/UiSounds.cpp`; no third-party recording is bundled.
